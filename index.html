MOVIE NIGHTS

Sinema

Cushions, blankets, and a classic on the big screen.

A cosy indoor screening where everyone sits on carpets, cushions and blankets. Phones go in the box, the lights go down, and the film does the ice-breaking. Strangers walk in, and by the credits they are arguing about the ending together.

DURATION
4 hours · 5–9 PM
SETTING
Indoors, floor seating
INCLUDED
Coffee or hot chocolate + donut
ENTRY
From Rs 2,500
HOW THE NIGHT GOES
01 Check in and drop your phone in the box
02 Grab your coffee, donut and a cushion spot
03 Watch the film together, popcorn at the venue
04 Stay for the post-movie chat and new friends

Case Night

Someone at this table is a killer.

You get seated with strangers, handed a case file and a character. Clues, suspects and red herrings are hidden through the evening. Interrogate your table, trade evidence, and name the killer before your coffee gets cold.

3.5 hours
TABLE

03 Hunt clues, question suspects, swap evidence
04 Accuse the killer, then antakshari and board games

Loud, competitive, zero Wi-Fi.

Doubles with strangers. Rackets up, phones down.

Social padel sessions where partners are drawn at random. Beginners and regulars play together, rotate courts and end the evening with chai at courtside. Same rule as always: come alone, leave with a squad.

2–3 hours
FORMAT
Random doubles, rotating
LEVEL
All levels welcome
